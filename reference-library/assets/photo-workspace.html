Capture with purpose

More angles. Fewer assumptions.

  1. Go around the part. Include front, back, left, right, top and bottom when accessible.
  2. Show what must fit. Add close views of holes, interfaces and hidden features.
  3. Measure a reference. Place a ruler in the same plane as the feature you want to estimate.

View labels help reveal gaps. They do not verify photo quality or prove 3D accuracy.
