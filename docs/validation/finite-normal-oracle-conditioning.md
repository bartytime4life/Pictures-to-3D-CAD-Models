## Fixed comparison and criteria

All controls supply centre zero, radius one, fraction zero, normal `(1,0)`,
identity covariance and observation centre `(0.5,0.5)`. Their restricted maps
have the independent elementary integral derived in the method guide. The 24
new scenes cross denominator exponents 10, 18 and 24, two compressed-map offsets,
both denominator slopes and both determinant signs. The exact represented
offset is used, rather than its nominal decimal label.

The original two saved forests retain 32 and 18 panels. Every new scene uses
the same fixed 58-interval dyadic endpoint partition, covering the entire source
chord. No interval is omitted in this population. No panel, order, precision,
scene or threshold was adapted after evaluation.

The unchanged reference, endpoint interpolation and two Decimal paths reuse the
same newly generated arrays at orders 96 and 192. The Decimal paths and the
independent elementary reference use both 80- and 120-digit contexts. Every
reference and Decimal-result precision comparison meets the fixed mixed
`1e-65 * max(1, abs(result120))` stability criterion. Both original baseline totals reproduce the saved
V2 values **exactly in hexadecimal at both orders**. The old literal rule arrays
were not saved; exact total reproduction does not establish their original bytes.

For every scene/order, the exact-source Decimal and endpoint results meet

```text
abs(result-reference120) <= 1e-10 + 512*binary64_epsilon*abs(reference120).
```

This is the declared empirical comparison allowance, not a certified enclosure.
The two original endpoint controls additionally meet their original absolute
`1e-10` request at **both** orders. The new high-amplitude controls use the stated
relative term; a pass does not mean all errors are below `1e-10`.
