## Conditional two-witness consequence

For two verified alternatives with the same fixed numerical observation and
fraction coordinates f1 and f2, any common image-only output fhat satisfies

    |f1-f2| <= |fhat-f1| + |fhat-f2|
    max(|fhat-f1|, |fhat-f2|) >= |f1-f2|/2.

Minor PNG's extrema are actual captures (axis 6, slot 2, trial 45) and
(axis 2, slot 2, trial 17). Their exact half-separation is

    4493972296869/1125899906842624 > 1/500 = 0.002.

Over a numerical-model/domain class admitting BOTH seven-parameter alternatives,
one common estimate from their identical observation cannot have absolute
fraction error <=0.002 for both. This is a conditional two-point minimax lower
bound, not identification of either alternative as physical or synthetic truth.
All geometry coordinates and both unknown brightness levels may vary within
the original declared domain. Reserved truth/dimensions did not select them.

Middle PNG's smaller span establishes neither a 0.002 impossibility nor attainable
accuracy: only a subset of compatible points was sampled. No sampled span is an
outer bound, confidence interval, feasible interval or complete diameter.
Coordinate aliases prevent a generic five-tuple count from certifying distinct
physical shapes.

The saved actual equal-observation witnesses demonstrate ambiguity conditional
on the declared numerical model. They do not prove uniform failure of the
original 12-case benchmark, explain middle PNG's geometry error, recover
geometry, certify physical/camera ambiguity or uncertainty coverage, or show
that intermediate points are compatible. Original recovery remains 11/12,
with the original fraction target unresolved and its best unchanged.
