# Live inward and observed-defect restoration

This is a **prospective, unexecuted research strategy**. All new backend
math/controller code, independent controls, full-protocol adapter, admission
and this guide must be independently reviewed, committed and combined-hashed
with the preceding 116 governing files before any first new subject import,
collection, test, solve, point or model call. No new recovery score or
runtime qualification is claimed here.

The preceding saved-context experiment reduced actual training cost from
0.302031169350812 to zero for one supplied synthetic PNG case. Only source
62 changed code in that experiment. It did not rerun the original 24-case
protocol or identify unique geometry. This new strategy must derive its
inputs from a **fresh live fit**, without reading that saved point, optical
field, certificate or result.

## Integrate at the event that loses a represented point

The existing protected controller can retain an exactly qualified rational
certificate even when its floating-point projection is unavailable. The
new controller intercepts that live event before the old early continuation.
It starts with the exact unchanged coupled-v1 initial phase **once**, then
uses the existing protected event order, score census, encoder witnesses,
source bands and original final coordinate polish.

Only the first eligible exact certificate in a complete fit may trigger a
restoration episode. There is at most one inward nearest trial and, after a
fully resolved rejected trial, one defect-corrected nearest trial. There is
no case-name, row-family, source-62 or rank-five condition. Smooth, staged
and already-zero-cost paths retain their ordinary behavior.

Bind the current accepted physical tuple `p0`, its actual optical field `z0`,
the live seven-column matrix `G`, selected event and target code, raw source
bands, normalized bounds, physical limits and spans, exact point and dual,
and source/operator identities. Keep the accepted capture pinned. Reading
the rejected trial must not repin or replace it.

## One inward physical proposal

Reverify the exact certificate under its unchanged old local problem before
using its point. Certificate coordinates and duals are bounded exact
rational strings, not floats parsed through a lossy conversion.

Use the previously derived bounded inward construction on all original
halfspaces and the physical-domain rows. Retain its exact basis, Gram
witness, margins, ray interval, selected displacement and all row slacks.
Rank, fixed-coordinate, bit-cap or empty-ray obstruction is an event-local
unknown, not global infeasibility.

For the one constructed normalized displacement `d` form

~~~text
p_target = F(p0) + F(s) d,
p1 = nearest_binary64(p_target),
~~~

where `F` converts each stored binary64 operand to its exact rational value.
Take only this nearest physical tuple. No 128-corner census, cost-based
corner selection, clipping or alternate fallback is performed.

Require the original five gates: physical limits/fixed identity, exact
membership of the physical round trip, exact membership of computed
coordinates, computed source/domain membership, and selected-band arithmetic
floor. Exact and computed normalization remain distinct. The selected band
is its declared central-half proposal slice, not the whole encoder bin.

Evaluate a fully qualified `p1` through the **same live engine** and original
training queries, residual weights and own brightness levels. Accept only
a fully resolved strict actual-cost decrease relative to `p0`. Code-pattern
changes are diagnostics and do not veto an otherwise valid decrease.

## One correction through a rejected actual observation

Only a fully resolved, valid rejected `p1` supplies the actual optical field
`z1` for a correction. Keep `p0` as the acceptance reference. Use

~~~text
u = (p - p1) / s,
m1(u) = z1 + G u,
d1 = (F(p1) - F(p0)) / F(s).
~~~

Zero spans fix their coordinates. Check the original `p0`-normalized domain,
using exact operands before subtraction and division; do not round a shifted
intercept or box into another helper's interface.

Retain the first independent original tight normals in original row order
after removing fixed coordinates. Let their matrix be `B` and their new
signed bounds be `beta`. Solve exactly once:

~~~text
(B B^T) lambda = beta - storage_margin - representation_margin,
u = B^T lambda.
~~~

Keep every dependent, inactive and physical row for final verification.
The equality and row-space identities prove minimum Euclidean norm **only
for the selected equalities**: for `B h = 0`, `u^T h = 0` and
`||u+h||^2 = ||u||^2 + ||h||^2`. This is not a full inequality-QP optimum,
a transfer of old KKT optimality or a nonlinear convergence theorem.

Use the fixed source guard `2^-12` on nonstructural source rows and the
separate binary64 representation allowance. Structural rows receive no
strict margin. Numerical input bounds are checked; nearest rounding and
gradual underflow remain assumptions. The storage allowance does not bound
geometry-linearization remainder, mixed brightness terms, stale derivatives
or an unknown nonlinear pipeline.

Take one nearest physical tuple and require all final exact/computed/domain,
source and arithmetic-floor gates. No new inequality search, basis exchange,
retry, corner census or step-size choice occurs. Evaluate that point at
most once through the same live engine and compare strictly with `p0`,
not merely with rejected `p1`.

This design is informed by the working-set minimum-norm correction and
subsequent actual trial test in
[Byrd, Nocedal, Waltz and Wu, section 2.5](https://optimization-online.org/wp-content/uploads/2011/04/3003.pdf).
Their smoothness assumptions do not hold for this quantized chain. The
strategy does not identify a Maratos effect or inherit their convergence
results. The standard floating-point arithmetic model is described in
[Higham and Mary, section 2.2](https://eprints.maths.manchester.ac.uk/2780/1/paper.pdf);
their LU theorem is not claimed here.

## One shared live budget

The prospective study retains ordinary/default per-fit ceilings of **2,048
evaluations, 1,000,000 cell units and 100,000,000 stage units**, the original
iteration limits and each adverse case's lower declared limits. These are
study ceilings, not API validation maxima for caller budgets. Include
the coupled initial phase, all event work, both new constructions, actual
trials, captures and final coordinate polish in that one budget.

Before an episode, preflight the conservative worst-case work for both
mathematical constructions and two actual forwards. This is **affordability
only**, not an additional debit. An unaffordable optional episode may be
skipped while ordinary event or coordinate work remains affordable; that
skip alone must not falsely mark the whole engine exhausted.

Charge the entire declared bounded tariff for each invoked mathematical
construction **before** its rational arithmetic. No refund follows a rank,
bit, representation or all-row failure. Actual literal evaluations retain
the existing engine's before-operator reservation and actual-work handling.
Do not call the archived standalone paired-probe orchestrator, replay the
incumbent separately, restart budgets or debit nested work twice.

For `S` finite source-band sides, let `R=S+14` include normalized box rows
and `A=R+14` add physical-domain rows. Each invoked construction uses
`T=65536+512*(R+9*A)` stage units, with `A<=4096`. For `N` source cells,
`M` output pixels and kernel entry counts `Ko,Kp`, one actual PNG forward
uses `W=N*(1+Ko+4)+M*(16+Kp)` stage units. Worst-case episode
preflight is `[2,2*N,2*T+2*W]`, affordability only.

Capture has a separate cap of **1,048,576 numeric entries**, requested as
`2*(6*N+2*M)` for two trial calls, pre-cast coverage and seven available
stages. These are conservative accounting units and payload entries, not
elapsed time, total process memory or bit-complexity theorems. Uncalled
later operations remain unattempted, not completed or hidden zero-work calls.

Unknown actual work or protocol/source failure withholds qualification.
Retain reservation, charged, reported and unavailable work separately, with
reasons and every partially completed record.

## Preserve stopping, ambiguity and evidence

An accepted restored point becomes the next live incumbent. A later event
would require fresh actual optics and a freshly charged matrix. The
one-episode cap prevents another restoration; normal bounded event control
and the original final coordinate search remain available.

The selected best must belong to the original completed-search record,
without exhausted, opaque or completion-withheld work. Zero training cost
does not waive that gate. Keep tied candidates, flatness, undefined rounded
derivatives and unknown geometry rank. Tight-basis rank is not observation
rank. A quantized zero-cost point can still miss a geometric or conditional
reserved target.

Retain bounded episode context/proof and all available actual stages, code
payload references, candidates, rejected/unknown outcomes and search links.
Capture allocation is bounded and declared before execution; retain any
unavailable payload explicitly. No archival point is injected into a fresh
fit, and no reference or reserved dimension selects a proposal.
